# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS, one page, GitHub Pages (repo wvdham/vdhgroup), domain vdhgroup.nl. Same setup as the owner's other sites.

## Users

Primary (confirmed by Wouter, 09-10-2026): the face of a future company. Later partners, banks, the accountant and a future holding B.V. or office under this name should see a real group. Secondary: visitors who click "powered by VDH Group" on one of the sites.

## Product Purpose

VDH Group is Wouter van der Ham's umbrella over everything he has built and runs. The page shows the group and leads to each part. Success: a visitor understands within seconds that one maker stands behind a set of serious, distinct ventures, and can reach every part.

## Positioning

A one-person group rooted in fieldwork: an ecological consultancy, products built on that expertise, nature media and bird tools, all made by the same field ecologist and birder. The common root is time in the field, not a corporate structure.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four branches, eight parts: Ecologie (Van Der Ham Ecologie, HIEON), Producten (checkjequickscan, BatReel), Natuurmedia (NatuurSpeld, NachtvlindersNL, Invested Birding), Vogellab (Birds on the Spectrum).
- Het Orakel is never shown: no permission from its data sources for public use.
- No statuses, numbers, prices or turnover. Little text. Dutch, "wij" form.
- The group must not look like Van Der Ham Ecologie: no VDHE greens or VDHE mark as its basis, and VDHE is not presented as the centre. checkjequickscan and NatuurSpeld are separate from VDHE, but not separate from VDH Group.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VDH Group. Logo: four rounded leaves, three night blue #24324F and one light blue #8EC5E8; wordmark "VDH" heavy plus "GROUP" spaced. Wouter finds the logo and name very good; keep both.
- Logo files: `logos/vdh-group-liggend.svg`, `-wit.svg`, `vdh-group.svg`, powered-by variants.
- Each part keeps its own logo and identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- The parts' logos in `logos/`.
- No testimonials, client names or figures may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Business first: the page reads as a professional company, not as a nature site.
2. Every part keeps its own face; the group is the frame, not a uniform.
3. Restraint in words, conviction in form.

## Direction

Wouter (09-10-2026), after seeing a plain first version ("heel saai en amateuristisch"): a classic, clear but beautiful company site in the style of a good annual report. Not a concept piece. Then (09-10-2026): "ik wil niet allemaal natuurfoto's erop, ik wil het zakelijk en professioneel". No nature photography; the brand mark carries the page.
